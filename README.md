## About The Project

A personal Productivity Workspace & Dashboard built using Svelte, TypeScript, and Vite. Designed to keep me focused, organized, and synced with their external workflows.

### Key Features
* **20 Themes** : Including Tokyo Night, Catppuccin (Mocha, Latte, Macchiato, Frappe), Gruvbox, Everforest, Rose Pine, Nordic Ice, Dracula, One Dark Pro, and more for when you change your system theme for the gazillionth time.
* **Integrated Pomodoro Timer** : Live focus sessions linked directly to subjects with detailed history logs and weekly/monthly/yearly SVG charts.
* **Unified Planner & Timeline** : Schedule study blocks, exams, and stuff with custom colors and labels in a daily vertical timeline or monthly grid.
* **2-Way Google Calendar Sync** : Sync your planner events natively to and from Google Calendar.
* **Public Holidays** : Automatically fetches official public holidays from Google's database and displays them as compact tags that expand on click.
* **Google Drive Auto-Backup** : Keeps your device as the source of truth, performing silent, debounced background backups and startup restorations.
* **Obsidian Vault Linking** : Local note links and markdown folder path mapping persist across sessions.
* **GitHub-style Heatmap** : Visualizes your daily study productivity trends directly on the dashboard page.
* **Insulting Pokemon** : Randomly generated, uninspired, and unoriginal Pokemon characters that will roast you on your failures.
